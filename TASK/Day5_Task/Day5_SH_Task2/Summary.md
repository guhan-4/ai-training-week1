Employee Salary Analysis: Summary of Findings

Problem:
This analysis explores how employee salaries vary across departments and how salary relates to years of experience.

Data:
The dataset contains 8 employees, with each record including department, salary in rupees, city, and years of experience. Salaries range from ₹30,000 to ₹90,000.

Three key insights
1. Finance has the highest average salary: ₹90,000, based on one employee. IT averages approximately ₹67,667 across three employees.
2. Sales has the lowest average salary: ₹41,000 across two employees.
3. Salary generally rises with experience in this sample: The employee with 12 years of experience earns the highest salary, while the employee with 3 years earns the lowest.

Two recommendations
1. Compare salaries by job title, seniority, and experience before making pay decisions. Department averages alone may hide differences in roles and responsibilities.
2. Review salary progression for Sales employees and compare it with appropriate market benchmarks.

Limitations
The dataset is small, with only 8 employees, and department sizes vary from one to three people. It does not include job titles, seniority levels, working hours, or market salary benchmarks. The observed relationship between experience and salary is descriptive and does not show that experience causes higher pay.
